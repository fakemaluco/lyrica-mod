## Spicy Lyrics

The FanDroid lyrics presentation adapts animation concepts and spring-based
motion from [Spicy Lyrics](https://github.com/Spikerko/spicy-lyrics), by
Spikerko and contributors. Spicy Lyrics is distributed under the GNU Affero
General Public License version 3. FanDroid is consequently distributed under
the same license; see `LICENSE`.

## Fraktality Spring

The analytic spring used by the lyrics renderer is a Java adaptation of the
spring implementation credited by Spicy Lyrics to
[Fraktality/spr](https://github.com/Fraktality/spr), originally published
under the MIT License. Its copyright and permission notice remain applicable
to that implementation.

## Lyrics databases

Word-synchronised TTML is retrieved at runtime from the
[AMLL TTML Database](https://github.com/amll-dev/amll-ttml-db), whose database
content is published under CC0-1.0. When AMLL has no suitable track, FanDroid
can query the public [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net/) API for synchronized LRC.
No lyrics from either service are bundled with FanDroid.

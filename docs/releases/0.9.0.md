## New in 0.9.0

- The floating karaoke popup adapts its layout as you resize it. Lyrics, artwork, and controls keep their proportions in wide and tall layouts.
- The popup has an always-visible close button, even with the HUD popup closed. The host can end the room, and guests can leave it from the panel.
- The floating karaoke popup stays visible and interactive with F1.
- The mod now credits Fakiz as its author.
- Choose the JAR for your Fabric version of Minecraft, either 1.21.1, 1.21.11, or 26.2.

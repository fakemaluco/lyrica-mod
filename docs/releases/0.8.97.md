## New in 0.8.97

- New Lyrica icon and a visual README with installation and privacy information.
- Servers running Lyrica relay Music Presence directly and support `/karaoke pop-up`. Guests can accept on the panel to follow the host’s lyrics and, with Spotify connected, sync playback.
- Shared panels follow the host’s next tracks. Drag the bottom bar to move them, scroll while dragging to change distance, and drag the lower-right corner to resize each axis.
- Optional Windows audio ducking and Ji AFK playback integration, plus a translucent settings screen with an opening animation.
- Each download targets its matching Minecraft version. Choose the JAR for Fabric 1.21.1, 1.21.11, or 26.2.
